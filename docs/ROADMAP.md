# Technical roadmap

Baseline inspected: LibSaturn af86f8518f0d014f2a752ca43035d2278bc9bf8f. **Target stock Saturn, NTSC 320x224 at a stable 30 displayed FPS**, with gameplay physics at a fixed 60 Hz. The goal is an intentionally 30 FPS game, not a temporary concession on the way to 60 FPS. Display FPS has not been independently verified on hardware. PAL and optional RAM cart enhancements follow stock qualification. Prefer bounded data and fixed-point math; no dynamic allocation in frame hot paths.

Installed CMake package (LibSaturn::Saturn, libsaturn_configure_executable, libsaturn_add_disc); smoke test using saturn/app.h and VDP1 rectangle. Verify ELF/map, BIN/CUE, launch in Ymir and Mednafen. Pin the actual SDK revision in evidence.

Distinguish face counts, emitted VDP1 commands, frame submissions and field cadence.

## Performance target (decision: stable 30 FPS)

The project owner selected **30 FPS** as the definitive visual target on the stock Saturn, including completed and visually rich scenarios. Do not sacrifice the final course, textures, visual effects or readable Sonic animations merely to chase 60 FPS. A 60 Hz **fixed simulation** is retained for movement/collision/input, independent of the 30 Hz rendering cadence (nominally two simulation ticks per rendered frame).

- **Frame budget:** 33.33 ms per presented frame, *including* world/character skinning, camera, scene transform/sort, VDP1 submission/draw, HUD, audio contention and system overhead. Aim below roughly 28–30 ms in representative heavy scenes to leave a small 3–5 ms safety margin for spikes; that margin is a development goal, not a measured result.
- **Frame pacing:** prefer a regular two-field NTSC presentation cadence; investigate stutter, large p95/p99 frame times, dropped/late swaps and uneven camera motion before lowering the graphics budget.
- **Acceptance:** measure a route with dense scenery, maximum expected actors/effects, busy camera views, running/jumping and HUD enabled/disabled. Compare sustained cadence, frame-time distribution, face/command counts, VDP1/VDP2/WRAM budgets and physics replay determinism. Qualify on Ymir and Mednafen, and independently verify displayed frames on physical Saturn; the XTDB `submission_rate_hz` field is a **submission rate**, not proof of actual display FPS.
- **Current evidence:** the repository's Sonic R LOD-160 single-case Ymir idle probe passed at about 30 scene submissions/s, with 159 faces, 125 skinned vertices and 148 textures, but this is *not* a stress test of a finished level. The earlier greybox/proxy rendering at 60 submissions/s is historical headroom evidence, **not a target**.
- Prioritize stable gameplay and better visuals over speculative simplification. Only create or select more aggressive LODs when worst-case scene measurements or VRAM constraints show they are needed.

## Current acceptance candidate — animated Sonic + Jade Gully visual pass

- All 11 previously developed physics/replay cases are now re-gated with the **tracked 159-face Sonic R LOD**, not only with the proxy; the actor records SXAC v3 clip history (idle/run/takeoff/fall/landing), actor/physics state synchronization, HUD determinism and single-step animation.
- XFP1 adds a bounded 64-frame timing window for p50/p95/p99 and phase timings. The acceptance candidate requires at least 29 scene submissions/s, terminal frame <=40 ms, recent p95 <=38 ms, no dropped physics time and zero draw timeouts/late swaps. These are conservative emulator probes for the definitive 30 displayed FPS goal; only hardware/video observation can verify actual display FPS.
- Jade Gully now has a first render-only authored dressing layer: palm silhouettes, off-track rocks and a waterfall. Its collision ribbons have not changed. Do not count this as a textured, visually finished stage.
- **Status:** code and host tests published; full 11-case Sonic + scenic Ymir results, Mednafen comparison, performance under many actors and physical-Saturn acceptance are still pending. Do not claim P3/P4 complete from an idle PASS.

## Ymir acceptance — 2026-11-20: all 11 skinned-Sonic scenarios PASS

**Verified evidence:** game `d55dad6010d398916a54f018bd3d3801885406db`, LibSaturn `08ad63b5923c97e498078628192a64ad66ce50a2`, [Ymir evidence `dbbae8d`](https://github.com/celsowm/sonic-xtreme-saturn/tree/dbbae8dbcc1d2447af67bb446acd0cf8c3da1d92/latest), CI [success](https://github.com/celsowm/sonic-xtreme-saturn/actions/runs/44832905591). 11/11 cases and 5/5 cross-case comparisons passed with the tracked Sonic R LOD-160, including grounded/airborne motion, animation clip history, pause/single-step, HUD equivalence and curve routing. The Blender visual layer (palms, rocks, waterfall) compiled and rendered in the captures. No errors, late swaps, VDP1 timeouts or dropped simulated time were reported. `XFP1` measured **p50=33 ms, p95=34 ms, p99=34 ms** in the last 64-frame window of each case (short idle: 62); `XTDB` reported 30 scene submissions/s. Highest case p95 scene phase was 21 ms; VDP1 free texture VRAM was 264,232 bytes; no command-budget overrun (2048 capacity, max observed 281 world commands). These are *emulator probe measurements*, not a physical-Saturn verification of actual video/display frame rate, nor a finished-stage stress benchmark. 34 ms p95 is just above the theoretical 33.33 ms per 30 Hz frame, so headroom is narrow even though all measured acceptance gates passed.

Known limitations not covered by current PASS: the moving jump/resume case ends airborne/off-course; no end-to-end 3–5 minute run, full collision walls/loops, camera occlusion treatment, textured rocks/foliage, rings/springs gameplay, multi-actor stress test or actual hardware performance. Screenshot inspection finds sparse isolated props and missing surrounding terrain; visual authoring and camera quality remain priorities. Do not infer completed gameplay or seamless displayed cadence from these tests.

## P5: production
Use HLOD and sector residency, VDP2 sky/ground/water where appropriate, audio/music through existing music.h and disc scheduler, prefetch around camera routes. Measure contention during transitions; no required cart. Maintain per-pool memory ledger (WRAM-H/L, VDP1, VDP2, SCSP). Compare overdraw, command count, frame-time percentiles, audio underruns and dropped frames at repeatable route checkpoints. Validate hardware timing independently.

## Acceptance evidence
Host tests -> SH-2 build -> deterministic disc -> Ymir captured route -> Mednafen comparison -> physical Saturn. For every run record commit, tool versions, BIOS identifier (never distribute BIOS), inputs, screenshots, frame/field metrics and failures. Include the P1.5 debug HUD pages and telemetry snapshot; compare HUD disabled/enabled replay results. A 30 FPS target is not a result. No unsupported assets in public source or releases.
