# Research and asset policy

Research leads (not approval or verified compatibility):
- Sonic X-treme prototype art and Jade Gully preservation: https://models.spriters-resource.com/saturn/sonicxtreme/
- Sonic Jam: https://models.spriters-resource.com/saturn/sonicjam/
- Sonic Z-Treme: https://github.com/Maxime-XL2/SONIC-Z-TREME
- Sonic Adventure behavior/reference: https://github.com/reshaped/sonic-adventure
- SA tooling: https://github.com/X-Hax/SonicAdventureBlenderIO
- Sonic GT and Sonic World DX: level-design references.

Availability online is not permission to redistribute. No proprietary art, models, code, music, ROM, BIOS or unlicensed fan assets committed. No linking GPL components into this project without an explicit license/architecture decision. Use original proxies and independently authored gameplay. For each candidate record original author, source URL, retrieval date, hashes, license and explicit allowed uses. Keep any permitted local research inputs outside tracked paths; never automate scraping or bypass protections. Only demonstrably redistributable material can ship.
